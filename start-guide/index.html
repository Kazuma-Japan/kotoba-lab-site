    Start Guide

    This guide explains exactly how to use your tools and what to do on Day 1.

    Step 1 — Set Up Your Flashcards (5 minutes)

    Your flashcards are hosted on Quizlet — free on any device, no special app required.

    Open Your Quizlet Flashcard Set

    How to use Quizlet:

    1. Click the link above
    2. Create a free Quizlet account (or log in if you have one)
    3. Click "Study" → choose "Flashcards" mode
    4. On your phone: download the free Quizlet app (iOS or Android)

    Each card shows:

    • Front: Japanese phrase with reading in parentheses — e.g., 〜と申します（〜ともうします）
    • Back: English meaning + example scene + Day number

    Daily goal: 10–15 minutes per day, right before or after your lesson.

    Step 2 — Bookmark Your AI Practice Partner (2 minutes)

    Your AI speaking partner is available 24/7 at:

    https://kotoba-lab-chat.vercel.app

    Bookmark this on your phone and computer now. You'll use it every day.

    What it can do:

    • Have a natural conversation with you in Japanese
    • Switch to English if you're confused
    • Correct your Japanese naturally (without interrupting the flow)
    • Give detailed feedback on grammar, expressions, and structure when you ask
    • Act out the Day's scenario with you

    Step 3 — Open Day 1 and Begin

    Find Day 1 in the left sidebar and follow these 5 steps.

    Your Daily Routine (20–30 min)

    ① Read the Scenario (2 min)

    Each day starts with a real-life situation — a conversation at a Tokyo meetup, a convenience store, a job interview. Read it and picture yourself there.

    ② Study the Key Phrases (3 min)

    You'll get 6–8 phrases for that day. Don't memorize them yet — just read them out loud a few times and get them in your ear.

    ③ Listen to the Audio, Then Read the Model Conversation Out Loud (5 min)

    An audio file is included for each day. Listen first, then read it aloud yourself. This is the most important habit.

    The audio player appears above the conversation text on each Day page. Speaker A = Japanese character, Speaker B = English-speaking learner.

    ④ Do the Translation Sprint (10 min)

    English sentences appear one by one. Your job: say the Japanese out loud within 3 seconds.

    • Easy set: basic phrases from the day
    • Medium set: slightly longer sentences
    • Hard set: multi-clause, natural-sounding sentences

    Don't write. Don't look up answers. Just say it — even if it's wrong. The goal is to build reflex, not perfection.

    ⑤ Practice with the AI (10 min)

    At the end of each Day, there's an "AI Practice Template." Copy it and paste it into the AI chat at kotoba-lab-chat.vercel.app.

    The AI will immediately take on a character and start talking to you. Have the conversation. It's okay to make mistakes — that's exactly what this is for.

    Tip: After the conversation, type: "Please give me feedback on my Japanese."

    One Important Note

    You don't need to be able to read hiragana perfectly before Day 1.

    Each Japanese phrase includes a reading guide in parentheses — for example:

    • 毎日（まいにち） = "mai-ni-chi" = "every day"
    • 大丈夫（だいじょうぶ） = "dai-jou-bu" = "it's okay"

    However, learning hiragana in the first week will make everything much easier. It takes about 2–3 hours total. We recommend:

    Tofugu's Free Hiragana Guide — the best free resource

    You can do Days 1–3 before finishing hiragana, but try to have it done by Day 7.

    FAQ

    Q: How long does each day take? A: 20–30 minutes. If you're doing the AI conversation for the first time, add 10 extra minutes.

    Q: What if I miss a day? A: Just pick up where you left off. Don't double up — one lesson per day is enough.

    Q: I can't read the Japanese characters. What do I do? A: Every phrase has a reading guide in parentheses. Use those. Study hiragana as soon as possible.

    Q: The AI is responding in Japanese and I don't understand anything. A: Type "Please respond in English for now" and the AI will switch.

    Q: Can I use this on my phone? A: Yes. Everything works in any mobile browser.

    Q: Do I need to do the days in order? A: Yes — each Phase builds on the previous one.
