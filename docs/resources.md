## Tips for Success

1. **Do it every day** — 20 minutes daily beats 2 hours on weekends
2. **Speak out loud** — reading silently doesn't build speaking ability
3. **Don't skip the AI practice** — this is where real speaking happens
4. **Review flashcards on your commute** — phone + Quizlet app = easy wins
5. **It's okay to make mistakes** — the AI won't judge you
