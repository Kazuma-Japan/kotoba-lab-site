# Welcome to the 60-Day Japanese Speaking Challenge

**Congratulations on joining Kotoba Lab!**

You now have everything you need to go from "I studied Japanese but can't speak it" to actually having real conversations — in 60 days.

---

## What's Inside

- **60 Daily Lessons** — each one takes 20–30 minutes
- **Audio Files** — listen to model conversations spoken by native-level voices
- **336 Flashcards** — daily vocabulary review on Quizlet (free)
- **AI Practice Partner** — chat with an AI in Japanese, anytime, 24/7

---

## How to Start

1. Read the [Start Guide](start-guide.md) — takes 5 minutes
2. Open [Day 1](phase-a/day01.md) and begin

That's it. No setup, no downloads, no complicated instructions.

---

## The 4 Phases

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|-------|------|-------|
| **A — Foundation** | 1–15 | Self-introduction, daily life, basic conversations |
| **B — Daily Life** | 16–30 | Navigating Japan: trains, stores, restaurants, phones |
| **C — Expression** | 31–45 | Opinions, stories, culture, emotions |
| **D — Real World** | 46–60 | Keigo, workplace, nuance, internet language |

Each phase builds on the previous one. Do them in order.
